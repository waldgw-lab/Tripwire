---
name: Source provenance policy
description: Corpus analysis is unreviewed interpretation; offline backfills do not authorize live retrieval.
---

Treat source families as hypotheses about an information environment, never a
confidential human's identity. Claim families group the same proposition, not
every related topic. Article counts, source occurrences, provisional records,
anonymous-human lower bounds and reviewed evidence paths are separate measures.
Never add potentially overlapping anonymous groups into a false total.

**Why:** The user explicitly defined these conservative distinctions when
adapting the supplied Source Search corpus to Tripwire.

**How to apply:** Preserve supplied interpretations and external keys for review;
only exact retained passages receive validated excerpt references. Do not infer
an analyst's identity or promote supplied confidence into independence.

Article-linked claims from the user's supplied reporting must not require
routine claim verification or display as a queue of unreviewed chores.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected having to review and verify claims already
published or supported by the articles they supplied.

**How to apply:** Present their article attributions directly. Keep technical
provenance, optional lineage/independence assessments and supporting evidence in
closed, relevant disclosures. Do not equate this presentation with automatically
asserting a claim's truth or independent confirmation. Claim previews show the
date/date range and total article attributions rather than legacy verified/total
counters. Use square outlet logos and complementary muted status colors.

New retained reporting should enter Claims without repeated manual passage
connection or routine verification. Direction belongs to a claim about its
subject, not to every claim copied from the article's overall sentiment.

**Why:** The user asked to ensure the Claims process works with new content,
following their request for positive/negative claim treatment and automation.

**How to apply:** Preserve exact passages, uncertainty, attribution, corrections
and separate independence decisions. Do not confuse local processing of retained
text with permission for publisher retrieval or paid-model/private-content calls.

Offline archive backfills do not authorize ongoing publisher retrieval.

**Why:** The existing automatic retriever otherwise treats newly inserted
historical metadata as fresh work, outside the user's one-time offline scope.

**How to apply:** Keep archive-origin records out of automatic retrieval unless
the analyst explicitly requests an individual item. Preserve any already
retained content and leave existing live-monitoring authorization untouched.
