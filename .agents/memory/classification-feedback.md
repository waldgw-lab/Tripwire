---
name: Classification feedback boundaries
description: Analyst corrections are evaluation evidence, not global monitoring instructions.
---

Treat correction history as a compact audit trail, not an action queue. Default
to analyst actions and make automatic history optional; preserve internal audit
records but do not present unchanged effective ratings as rating changes.

**Why:** The user found the expanded feedback area noisy and without an evident
action, especially repeated automatic HIGH-to-HIGH and LOW-to-LOW entries.

**How to apply:** Keep the history collapsed initially, explain View/Restore,
and distinguish real value or provenance changes from assessment refreshes.
One article dismissal or correction must not create a global outlet, journalist,
entity or topic exclusion. A reason such as "Incorrect entity match" explains
the analyst's judgment; it is not permission to remove that entity from the
registry or rewrite unrelated articles.

**Why:** The user requires feedback to remain a reviewed calibration dataset,
not an unrestricted instruction channel into monitoring policy.

**How to apply:** Keep automatic suggestions separate from independent field
overrides. Preserve ambiguous historical origin honestly as unknown; do not
invent analyst names, prior deletion reasons, or verified sourcing judgments.
