---
name: Journalist attribution
description: Attribution and identity constraints for connecting journalists to outlet coverage.
---
An author or contributor role belongs to a person's credit on a specific article, not to their global journalist profile. A coauthored article belongs under each credited person, but counts once in outlet totals and once per person's coverage even when they hold multiple roles.

**Why:** The user explicitly noted multiple authors and separately distinguished contributing credits, and asked for thoughtful article–journalist alignment.

**How to apply:** Preserve original credit wording and display byline names without rearranging first and last names. Sort separately by surname. Do not infer contributor roles from mentions in article prose or conflate article contributions with claim-level sourcing.

Group coverage under the article's actual publisher, not whichever outlet appears most frequently in a journalist's history. A requested home-outlet affiliation can keep a tracked profile visible with no collected items but must not pull another outlet's articles into that outlet's nested coverage.

**Why:** Journalists can have coverage at multiple outlets; frequency is not proof of exclusive employment or affiliation.

**How to apply:** Filter nested coverage by publisher before counting or sorting recent coverage. Use explicit aliases for identity linking, not speculative fuzzy merges; retain original bylines even when profiles merge.
