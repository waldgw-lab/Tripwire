---
name: Evidence version context
description: Distinguish retained wording from article selection without rewriting historical evidence.
---
Treat source-version scope as a statement about exact retained wording, never about truth, completeness, or independent reporting.

**Why:** A cleaned page or shorter selected copy can omit earlier reporting, and the same passage can survive in selected text at different offsets. Historical claim totals can legitimately exceed the selected processing run.

**How to apply:** Validate quotations against their original capture and preserve those offsets and reviewed evidence links. Check exact wording against selected text separately. Keep unmatched imported/legacy claims explicitly uncertain rather than dropping them. Retain article-wide historical counts when filtering provenance and explain their scope.
