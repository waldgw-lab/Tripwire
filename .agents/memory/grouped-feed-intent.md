---
name: Grouped Feed intent
description: Default chronology, lossless optional grouping and separation from Claims and bias judgments.
---

Keep the chronological Feed as the default. The optional Grouped view organizes
current reporting by development, complementary to Claims, without omitting
articles that have no extracted claims or enriched content. Existing explicit
filters, including default Low exclusion, still apply.

**Why:** The user asked for relevance/recency-based grouped reporting without
restricting the Feed to content that has reached Claims processing.

**How to apply:** Treat grouping as a display/navigation layer, not article
identity or evidence corroboration. Preserve original headlines and access to
every eligible article, including uncertain singletons. Outlet breadth is not
independent sourcing. Do not infer political-bias scores from outlet identity.
