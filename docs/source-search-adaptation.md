# Reporting provenance adaptation

## Governing workflow

Article identity is its exact canonical publisher URL, not a package ID or a
similar headline. A retained capture is an immutable version of user-supplied
text. An occurrence records the wording used by that article/capture; a reused
external Source ID does not merge occurrences. A passage can support an
article-attributed proposition. Claim families group the same proposition;
event groups provide related coverage without asserting equivalence.

Source-environment families and pairwise matches are information-environment
hypotheses, never confidential-person identities. Imported matches, access
profiles, family memberships and independence assessments remain supplied,
unreviewed analysis. No journalist owns a source.

## Counts and review

Article counts, source occurrence counts, provisional source-record counts,
minimum distinct anonymous humans and independently assessed evidence paths
are different measures. Unknown is not zero. “Officials” implies at least two
within that attribution, “one of them” adds none, and “another” requires a
separate person. Across overlapping or potentially aliased groups, do not sum
lower bounds. A sentence may have several attributions without multiplying
claims or independent paths. Supplied workbook totals are displayed as
supplied, not computed verified counts.

Each independence decision needs exact supporting evidence, a rationale and
an explicitly reviewed path key. Different outlets, bylines or descriptors
alone are insufficient. New reporting, added evidence, derivative repetition,
opinion propagation, disputes and unknown lineage are separate dispositions.
Earliest observed coverage is not verified origin. Missing bodies and
unmatched quotations cannot establish evidence or independence.

## Compatibility and correction

Existing Claims and their observations remain intact, including legacy
verified flags. Those flags are historical assessments, not newly validated
passages or independent paths. Rich source observations reference the same
claim record. Legacy observations are reconciled by article/claim without
inventing a capture, passage, review or analyst name. Entity development
evidence links to these same records, rather than asserting verification a
second time. Corrections append revisions and retain prior decisions.

## Backfill boundaries

This is a bounded, offline, development-only operation, not an app importer,
provider call, publisher scraper or monitoring activation. All workbook rows
and narrative documents retain input hashes and row/paragraph provenance.
Unclear URLs, inconsistent outlets, conflicting titles and unresolved joins
are quarantined. Shared public metadata is not rewritten; matching workspace
overrides preserve curation, hidden state, IDs and publication evidence.
Source archives are user-supplied text, never newly publisher-verified.
Yearless timestamps do not acquire a guessed year or timezone. Author Group
does not promote contributors to authors. Narrative summaries and the mixed
Max Boot synthesis are not complete publisher articles.

Application is transactionally journaled with before/after images and an
input hash. Rerunning identical applied inputs is a no-op. Rollback restores
only unchanged batch-owned values; subsequent edits or dependent records
block rollback instead of being deleted.
